<p>SpeakerLink helps you use your Bluetooth speakers and headphones, one at a time or several together.</p>

<p><b>What it does</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Lists your paired speakers, headphones and earbuds, finds new ones in pairing mode and pairs them.</li>
<li>Keeps several devices connected at the same time and shows which one is playing.</li>
<li>Moves the sound to another connected device with one tap.</li>
<li>Together: shows what your phone can do to play on several devices at once, such as Samsung Dual Audio or Android Audio sharing with LE Audio headphones and speakers, and explains speakers that link with each other.</li>
<li>Test sound to check where the audio comes out.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Extras for JBL speakers</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Recognises the model from its name or Bluetooth advertisement.</li>
<li>Linking planner: tells you which of your JBL speakers can play together (PartyBoost, Auracast, Connect+ or PartyBox TWS), picks a host speaker and walks you through linking them.</li>
<li>Battery level, firmware and a locate sound on many JBL portables (beta, uses JBL's unofficial control protocol).</li>
</ul>

<p><b>How playing on several devices works</b></p>
<p>Most Android phones send audio to one Bluetooth device at a time. Some can do more: Samsung phones play on two Bluetooth audio devices at once with Dual Audio, and some phones with Android 16 can share audio with LE Audio (Auracast) headphones and speakers. Many speakers can also link with each other: your phone plays on one of them and the others get the music from it. SpeakerLink shows which of these your phone and your devices support.</p>

<p><b>Notes</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Connecting devices from inside the app uses Android Bluetooth functions that are not part of the public SDK. If your phone blocks them, SpeakerLink opens the system Bluetooth settings instead.</li>
<li>Android 12 and higher asks for the "Nearby devices" permission. On Android 11 and lower, finding new devices requires the location permission. SpeakerLink never uses your location.</li>
<li>No internet permission, no ads, no tracking.</li>
</ul>
