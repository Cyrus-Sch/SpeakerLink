# Changelog

Changes to SpeakerLink for Mac. Each version is released from the tag `mac-v<version>`, and the
release notes are taken from its section below.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Play the same audio on several Bluetooth headphones and speakers at once, of any brand, together
  with the Mac's own speakers or other outputs if you like.
- Menu bar app: tick the devices and choose Listen together. Every app then plays on all of them.
- Volume for each device, because macOS's volume keys can't control a multi-output device.
- Sync delay for each device, with test clicks to line the devices up by ear.
- Connect paired Bluetooth headphones and speakers from the menu.
- Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
- No network access and no analytics.
